Rayleigh Backscatter and Phase Sensitivity in Φ-OTDR
Φ-OTDR uses coherent Rayleigh backscatter as a distributed interferometer, converting tiny phase shifts from vibrations into detectable intensity changes. A highly coherent laser pulse (narrow linewidth <100 kHz) is launched into the fiber, yielding Rayleigh backscattered light from each microscopic scatter site
