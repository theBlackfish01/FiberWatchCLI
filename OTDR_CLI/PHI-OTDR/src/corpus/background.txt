Background — Low-RMS, Low-Kurtosis Noise Floor

Background in Φ-OTDR/DAS is dominated by low-level, mostly stationary noise with weak spatial coherence and near-Gaussian amplitude statistics.
Signature summary. Temporal envelope: steady or slowly drifting; bursts possible under wind/rain. Typical duration: persistent; evaluate in 5–20 s windows. Recurrence: none. Spectral bands: energy often <100 Hz; traffic may add tones/harmonics up to a few hundred Hz. Spatial spread: speckled across many channels without a moving front. Amplitude stats: low mean and RMS; kurtosis ≈3 (Gaussian) except during gusts/showers.

Differentiators. No clear lateral motion (vs. walking); no periodic carrier (vs. shaking); no impulsive, high-kurtosis spikes (vs. knocking/digging); no sustained broadband hiss at one location (vs. watering/leaks).
Operational context & false positives. Wind and rain increase low-frequency content and inter-channel correlation; nearby traffic and pumps introduce semi-tonal lines. Poor coupling/loose slack sections can mimic low-RMS “quiet” lanes. Treat background as a diverse negative class; include varied weather/traffic snapshots when curating negatives to control NAR.
