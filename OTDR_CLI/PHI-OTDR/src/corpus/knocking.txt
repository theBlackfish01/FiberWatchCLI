Knocking — Short, Sparse, High-Kurtosis Transients

Knocking manifests as a small cluster of sharp impulses at one spot, with little or no tail and high spectral bandwidth.
Signature summary. Temporal envelope: 1–10 short hits in < 5–15 s. Recurrence: irregular (0.5–2 Hz typical). Spectral bands: broad, often 20–200 Hz (site-dependent). Spatial spread: 1–3 adjacent channels. Amplitude stats: very high kurtosis; elevated RMS only during hits; mean returns quickly to baseline.

Differentiators. Vs. digging: fewer repetitions, no soil backflow “hiss” tail; shorter total duration. Vs. walking: no diagonal propagation; footsteps show quasi-periodic pacing and move across channels. Vs. shaking: lacks continuous carrier.
Operational context & false positives. Hand taps on fence posts/cable trays; tool knocks for testing. False positives: hail/large raindrops on surface-laid fiber; loose clamps rattling once or twice. Use burst-count (≤ 10), inter-hit interval variance, and kurtosis thresholds to separate from extended excavation sequences.
