Shaking vs. Watering vs. Background — Quick Spectral Heuristics

A simple PSD triage often separates these: tonal (shaking) vs. broadband localized (watering) vs. broadband diffuse (background).
Signature summary. Compute (per channel/window) peak-to-median PSD ratio (PMR), spectral flatness (SF), and spatial compactness (SC = fraction of energy within ±2 channels). Shaking: high PMR (≥ 6–10 dB), low SF, high SC; Watering: low PMR, higher SF, high SC; Background: low PMR, moderate SF, low SC.
Canonical heatmap cues. Shaking: stationary wavy bands; Watering: solid block at one location; Background: speckle.
Operational differentiators. Require stationarity (position variance ≤ 1 channel) to rule out walking. For borderline cases, examine band-entropy between 0–20 Hz vs. 20–200 Hz: watering tends to fill both; shaking concentrates.

Digging vs. Knocking vs. Walking — Impact Train Logic

Use impact count, cadence regularity, and spatial drift to resolve the most common confusion trio.
Heuristics. Count discrete peaks above an adaptive threshold (e.g., median + k·MAD). If count ≤ 5 and duration < 10 s at one location → knocking. If count ≥ 10, inter-hit CV < 0.3, and stationarity (drift < 1 channel) → digging. If monotonic channel index slope with step-rate 1–3 Hz → walking. Add kurtosis gate (> 4 favors digging/knocking).
Why it works. Soil “backflow” and tool handling extend digging sequences and produce semi-periodicity; footsteps move spatially; taps are sparse and irregular.

References: [1] Cao et al., 2023, Results in Optics 10:100372; [2] Chen et al., 2023, Electronics 12:3757; [3] Luckie et al., 2025, SRL 96:2463.

Per-Class Feature Cheats (Summary Table & Threshold Ideas)

Apply simple, explainable thresholds first; let ML refine margins.

Background: RMS < μ+2σ; kurtosis ~2.5–3.5; SC < 0.2.

Digging: impact rate 0.5–3 Hz; ≥ 10 impacts/10 s; kurtosis > 4; stationarity drift < 1 ch.

Knocking: 1–10 impacts/10 s; inter-hit CV > 0.3; kurtosis > 5; stationarity drift < 1 ch.

Shaking: PMR ≥ 6–10 dB at 2–10 Hz; SF low; SC ≥ 0.5; zero-crossing regularity high.

Watering: PMR < 3 dB; SF high; SC ≥ 0.5; broadband energy present 0–200 Hz.

Walking: step rate 1–3 Hz; monotonic spatial slope; inter-channel coherence peak at lag matching pace.

References: [1] Liu et al., 2022, Opto-Electronic Adv.; [2] Stajanca et al., 2018, Sensors; [3] Peng et al., 2020, Sci. Rep.; [4] BJTUSensor GitHub (dataset).

Class-Specific Operational Notes (Field Examples & Pitfalls)

Tie lab patterns to field reality to reduce NAR.

Background: include windy/rainy days, pump cycles, and quiet nights in negatives.

Digging: genuine excavation persists; verify with stationarity + impact cadence; beware hammering on nearby structures.

Knocking: often tests/curiosity; set alert debounce so 1–2 taps don’t alarm.

Shaking: can be maintenance checks or machinery; confirm narrowband peak & coherence before alarming.

Watering: hose/sprinkler; leaks show similar but may introduce structural resonances; check for persistent recurrence over hours/days.

Walking: benign; use geo-fences to suppress alarms in known footpaths.

References: [1] Stajanca et al., 2018, Sensors 18:2841; [2] Liu et al., 2022, Opto-Electronic Adv.; [3] OptaSense pipeline monitoring note (2018 PDF).
