Watering — Sustained Broadband “Hiss” at a Fixed Point

Watering generates continuous broadband energy localized at one position, often resembling leak- or spray-induced vibrational noise.
Signature summary. Temporal envelope: steady (≥ 5 s, often tens of seconds). Recurrence: none; amplitude may flutter with flow. Spectral bands: broadband low-to-mid frequencies; site-specific peaks from structure resonances; expect energy mainly < 200–500 Hz for soil/pipe-coupled cases. Spatial spread: tight (1–4 channels) unless water spreads along a surface. Amplitude stats: elevated mean/RMS; low-to-moderate kurtosis (< 3.5) vs. impulsive classes.

Differentiators. Vs. shaking: lacks a narrow spectral line; PSD is flatter (high band-entropy). Vs. knocking/digging: no discrete spikes or regular cadence. Vs. rain: watering is highly localized; rain shows many weak spots over distance.
Operational context & false positives. Hose/sprinkler near fiber; leaks/spew on pipelines; nearby air bleed or gas vent. Use band-energy ratios (broadband/low-band), spectral flatness, and spatial compactness to distinguish from tonal machinery and from distributed precipitation.
